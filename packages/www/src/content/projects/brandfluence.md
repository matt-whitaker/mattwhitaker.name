Helped an unfortunately now defunct super early stage start-up with a proposed marketing campaign agreement with
Aeropostale. Working with their temporary designer, rapidly prototyped and built this little mobile-focused application,
the purpose of which was to facilitate the submission of Instagram posts which contained Aeropostale products for possible
coupon code awards.

The app is a simple workflow, with most of the focus on branding and flash, to allow a user to submit an Instagram post,
receive a coupon code (driven by AI analysis), and have it sent to their email address.

**Best viewed on mobile, app is width-pinned on desktop**
